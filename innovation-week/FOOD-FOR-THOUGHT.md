# Food for Thought — Prompts from POCs 01–06

*DRAFT, 2026-11-19. Prompts to think with, not tasks with right answers.
Several follow the questions each Month 1 film closed on.*

## By POC

**01 · An Agent That Sees**
- Which image does your work rely on that nobody checks region by region?
- Where would a hidden guess cost you most?
- Your threshold is 0.60. Who chose it, and what would change at 0.80?

**02 · Conversation Is a Coordination Primitive**
- If your agents' transcript were the only record, what could you not reconstruct today?
- Which finding would you want from data you never thought to ask about?
- What would make a transcript trustworthy in a dispute: order, signatures, retention?

**03 · RODS**
- Write the four lines for one agent you already use. Which line was hardest?
- What is your agent's signal? Could it be said by accident?
- Which Do-Not would you add after your worst incident?

**04 · The Analyst–Executor Loop**
- When did an empty result fool you into thinking there was nothing there?
- What is your team's "print the schema first" habit?
- Where does your code run when an agent writes it? Is that a real boundary?

**05 · Three Ways to Run a Group Chat**
- Which parliament does your team run today: a queue, a chair or a corridor?
- What does your chair spend on routing that nobody counts?
- If a run is capped, how would you know its output is wrong?

**06 · The Debate Pattern**
- Which recent decision arrived as one polished opinion?
- What rule would your judge apply, written down before the argument?
- Where would you keep the strongest losing argument?

## Across POCs
- Which of your processes would fail an audit because a hand-off is not written down? (02 + 05)
- Could a contract gate (03) have stopped a past incident before it ran?
- If your analyst agent (04) and your debate (06) disagreed, who decides, and on what record?
- Your confidence map (01) and your judge (06) both rank doubt. When should they be combined?
- What is the smallest real, recorded run that would turn one of your ILLUSTRATIVE figures into a MEASURED one?

## For leaders
- Which agent in your organisation has no written contract?
- Where is the human gate, and who staffs it?
- Which of your figures could not be moved by changing the data?
- What would you stop funding if the survival rate came back low?

## For beginners
- Think of a helper app you use. What does it promise, in four lines?
- When has a confident answer turned out wrong? How could it have said "I'm not sure"?
- In a group chat, who decides who speaks next?
